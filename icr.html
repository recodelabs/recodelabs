INTEGRATED CAMPAIGN REGISTRY

Every health campaign should build on the last

Campaigns reach the people routine services miss, but most start from scratch: a fresh pre-census, new lists, and tools that don't share data. The Integrated Campaign Registry (ICR) is an open data standard that keeps one shared record of places, households and teams, so each round starts from what the last one learned.
