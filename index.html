§ 02 · METHOD

How we work.

FOUR STAGES · REPEAT
STAGE · 01

Start from the problem.

Work with partners who can make a difference. Identify and validate the key problems worth solving.

STAGE · 02

Build in the open.

Leverage and contribute back to open source. Collaborate openly with partners and the community.

STAGE · 03

Fail fast.

Test with end users and partners early. Iterate quickly, adapt, and leverage AI to learn and self-improve.

STAGE · 04

Ship, or retire.

We replicate in a second context or we stop. We only focus on solutions with a pathway to scaled impact.
